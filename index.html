Who We Are

Building trusted partnerships through professional service.

Wijaya Partners Sdn Bhd provides collection services for organisations that value customer relationships. We work closely with our business partners through regular reporting, timely follow-up and service delivery aligned with their business objectives.

Our Vision

To achieve strong returns, service and satisfaction through innovative and reasonable financial solutions while maintaining integrity, professionalism and performance.

Our Mission

To maximise recovery in a timely, efficient, appropriate and professional manner while representing our business partners with care and commitment.

Our Approach

People. Process. Performance.

We focus on client relationships, effective account management, regular updates and continuous service improvement.

Our operations are supported by collection management systems and structured procedures designed to provide visibility and control.

What We Do

Our Core Services

Solutions supporting different stages of the credit and receivables lifecycle.

01 / RECOVERY

Debt Collection

Professional management of active, inactive and challenging accounts, with tailored follow-up and recovery strategies.

02 / CREDIT

Credit Management
